1. Future Simple

UZBKelajak haqida gapiramiz. Formula juda oddiy: will + fe’l. Hamma shaxs uchun bir xil! RUSГоворим о будущем. Формула простая: will + глагол. Для всех лиц одинаково!
+ I will call you.   − I won’t (will not) call.   ? Will you call?

Qachon ishlatamiz? / Когда используем?

1) Decision now · Hozir qaror · Решение прямо сейчас

“It’s cold.” “I will close the window.” — Sovuq. Men derazani yopaman. / Холодно. Я закрою окно.

2) Promise · Va’da · Обещание

I will help you. — Men sizga yordam beraman. / Я тебе помогу.

3) Prediction · Bashorat · Предсказание

It will rain tomorrow. — Ertaga yomg‘ir yog‘adi. / Завтра будет дождь.

Belgilar / Маркеры: tomorrow, next week, soon, tonight, in 2030, I think…

2. As much as

UZBas much as = teng miqdorda, xuddi shunchalik. Ikki narsani tenglashtiramiz. RUSas much as = столько же, настолько же. Мы сравниваем два действия как равные.

Formula: Subject + verb + as much as + Subject + verb

I trust my wife as much as I trust my mother.

Men ayolimga onamga ishonganchalik ishonaman. / Я доверяю жене так же сильно, как маме.

I don’t work as much as I used to.

Men hozir oldingidek ko‘p ishlamayman. / Я не работаю так много, как раньше.

3. Two more structures

A) Not as much as I would love to

UZB“O‘zim xohlaganchalik emas.” Qilaman, lekin xohlagandan kamroq.RUS«Не так много, как мне бы хотелось.» Делаю, но меньше, чем хочу.

I love football, but I don’t play it as much as I would love to.

Futbolni sevaman, lekin o‘zim xohlaganchalik o‘ynamayman. / Люблю футбол, но играю меньше, чем хотелось бы.

B) Either

UZBeither = ikkalasidan biri. Ikkita tanlov bor, bittasini olamiz. Inkorda: “hech qaysisi”.RUSeither = любой из двух / один из двух. С отрицанием: «ни один из двух».

You can take either tea or coffee.

Choy yoki qahva — bittasini oling. / Можно взять или чай, или кофе.

I don’t like either option.

Hech qaysisi yoqmaydi. / Мне не нравится ни один вариант.

4. Reading: Trip to Samarkand

Bold words are clickable: tap to learn. / Нажмите на жирные слова.

Next month Aziz and I will take a long journey to Samarkand, and I am already very excited. Aziz made a detailed itinerary, and he says we will visit all the famous places in three days. Our departure is at 7 a.m., so I will pack my luggage tonight. I will check my passport twice, because I don’t want any problems. At the station, the boarding starts thirty minutes before the train leaves. If there is a delay, we won’t panic. We will drink tea and wait patiently. The train fare is cheap, and I like trains as much as I like planes.

For accommodation we can choose either a hotel or a guesthouse. Honestly, I don’t like either option, because the prices are high, so we will stay with Aziz’s uncle. He lives near the Registan, and he promised that he will cook plov for us every evening. Before we go, I will exchange some currency. I love shopping, but I don’t spend as much as I would love to. My salary is small, and I don’t save as much as I used to.

Our final destination is the old city. On the first day we will arrive early in the morning and have breakfast. We will try the local food, because I think it will be delicious. During sightseeing we will walk through the old streets and take many photos. I will buy a souvenir for my mother, and Aziz will probably buy a small ceramic plate. Will we have enough time to see everything? I think we will, if we wake up early. I love hiking in the mountains, but I don’t go as much as I would love to, so maybe we will take a short trip to the hills too. On the last afternoon we can visit either the observatory or the museum. Honestly, either one will be interesting.

Our families will worry about us, but they will be happy when they see our photos. I trust Aziz as much as I trust my brother, so I know it will be amazing. I won’t forget this adventure, and next year we will travel again!
